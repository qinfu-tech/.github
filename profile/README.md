### About Us

**Qinfu Tech** is an independent software studio based in Shanghai, China.

We build practical, thoughtfully designed applications that make everyday life a little easier — from healthier work habits to real-time communication and personal finance.

We believe software should be **simple, reliable, and genuinely useful.**

### Our Products

#### iCadence — Break Reminder

*Move more. Work better.*

A break reminder app for people who spend long hours at their desks.

Custom schedules, persistent reminders, and activity confirmation help turn break reminders into real movement.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6751627034)

---

#### TransFloat — Live Translator

*Understand speech across languages.*

Real-time speech transcription and bilingual translation for conversations, videos, and supported audio sources.

Follow spoken content, review translations, and communicate across language barriers.

[Explore TransFloat](https://www.qinfu-cloud.cn/TransFloat/)

---

#### JointWorth — Family Net Worth

*Track your wealth, not every expense.*

A personal and family net worth tracker built around periodic financial reviews rather than daily expense logging.

Track assets, liabilities, multiple currencies, and shared household finances.

[Official Website](https://family.qinfu-cloud.cn/en/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757951397)
